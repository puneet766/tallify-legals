Account Deletion

Applies to: GrowTall: Height Increase AI (the "GrowTall" app on the Apple App Store and Google Play) Last updated: August 6, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your GrowTall account and the data associated with it. You can delete your account whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open GrowTall and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. In the Data section, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have GrowTall installed (or can't sign in), you do not need to reinstall it.

Email buddhabliss99@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my GrowTall account", sent from the email address your GrowTall account is registered under (or include that address in your message so we can locate the account).

We will verify the request and permanently delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by reply once it is done.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and sign-in identity (email, and name if you signed in with Apple or Google)
  • Your onboarding answers (date of birth, height, weight, parents' heights, lifestyle answers)
  • Your height, weight, sleep, and daily activity logs
  • Your nutrition plans, meal swaps, and AI-generated meals
  • Your subscription state records held by us (the subscription itself is managed and cancelled through Apple or Google — deleting your account does not cancel an active store subscription; do that in your store's subscription settings)

Your community posts and comments are permanently disassociated from your identity and displayed as "[deleted user]", so that other people's conversations are not destroyed.

What may be retained

We may retain limited records where the law requires it (for example, purchase and tax records processed by Apple or Google) and minimal fraud-prevention data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
